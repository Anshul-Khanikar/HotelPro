System Requirements
Instructions
HotelPro is a modern Hotel Management System designed to streamline hotel operations through a secure, scalable, and user-friendly platform. The system enables guests to search and book rooms, manage reservations, and process payments seamlessly, while hotel staff can efficiently handle room inventory, guest services, and daily operations. Administrators oversee hotel management, staff activities, reports, and system security. Built using the MERN Stack, HotelPro delivers a reliable, responsive, and scalable solution for hotels ranging from boutique properties to large hospitality chains.
